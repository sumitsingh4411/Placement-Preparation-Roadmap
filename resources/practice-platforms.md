## 🧮 DSA & coding

<div align="center">

| Platform | Use it for | Free tier |
|---|---|---|
| **[LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/)** ⭐ | **Your primary platform.** Company tags, contests, discussions | Excellent. Premium optional |
| **[GeeksforGeeks Practice](https://practice.geeksforgeeks.org/)** ⭐ | Indian company-specific questions, theory alongside | Free |
| **[HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/)** | **Many OAs run here — learn the interface** | Free |
| **[CodeChef](https://www.codechef.com/)** | Indian contests, good beginner ladders | Free |
| **[Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/)** | Competitive programming (only if you enjoy it) | Free |
| **[InterviewBit](https://www.interviewbit.com/)** | Structured, timed, interview-shaped | Free |
| **[AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp/)** | Clean problem quality, good contests | Free |
| **[Codewars](https://www.codewars.com/)** | Gamified, good for language fluency | Free |
| **[Exercism](https://exercism.org/)** | Language mastery with human mentorship | Free |

</div>

> [!TIP]
> **Practise on HackerRank at least a few times before your first OA.** A large share of Indian company assessments run on HackerRank or HackerEarth, and their editors, input parsing and submit behaviour differ from LeetCode's. Fighting the interface during a timed test costs real marks.

## 🎤 Mock interviews

<div align="center">

| Platform | What it is |
|---|---|
| **[Pramp](https://www.pramp.com/)** ⭐ | Free peer-to-peer mock interviews. Do these weekly |
| **[interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/)** ⭐ | Free anonymous mocks with engineers from top companies |
| **[Exponent](https://www.tryexponent.com/)** | Practice + peer matching (some free content) |
| **Friends / Discord** | Trade mocks. Free and effective |
| **Record yourself** | Solve a problem out loud, watch it back. Painful, extremely effective |

</div>

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do at least 10 mock interviews before your real ones.** The first time you feel interview nerves should not be in an interview that matters. Nerves are trainable — they fade with reps.

## 🏆 Contests & hiring challenges

<div align="center">

| Contest | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **LeetCode Weekly / Biweekly** ⭐ | Every week | Timed practice under pressure. Free |
| **CodeChef Starters** | Weekly | Beginner-friendly Indian contests |
| **Codeforces Div 3 / Div 4** | Frequent | Speed and problem-solving |
| **[TCS CodeVita](https://www.tcscodevita.com/)** ⭐ | Annual | Global contest with a direct interview path |
| **[Unstop challenges](https://unstop.com/)** ⭐ | Ongoing | Company hiring challenges — **much less competition** |
| **[HackerEarth challenges](https://www.hackerearth.com/challenges/)** | Ongoing | Company-sponsored hiring contests |
| **[Google Kickstart / Code Jam archive](https://codingcompetitions.withgoogle.com/)** | Archived | Practice problems from Google |
| **[Meta Hacker Cup](https://www.facebook.com/codingcompetitions/hacker-cup)** | Annual | Top performers get contacted |

</div>

> [!TIP]
> **Do LeetCode weekly contests even if you rank badly.** Contest conditions — a clock, unfamiliar problems, pressure — are the closest free simulation of an OA that exists. Your rank doesn't matter; the reps do.

## 📊 Tracking your progress

| Tool | For |
|---|---|
| **[LeetCode profile](https://leetcode.com/)** | Built-in stats and heatmap |
| **Google Sheets** | Your own problem log with revision dates → [templates/weekly-tracker.md](../templates/weekly-tracker.md) |
| **Notion** | Full study system, if you like structure |
| **GitHub** | Push your solutions — doubles as commit activity |
| **[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)** | Spaced repetition for CS theory and patterns |

**Minimum viable tracking:** a sheet with `Problem · Pattern · Date solved · Redo in 7 days · Redo in 30 days · Confident?`. That's all you need.
