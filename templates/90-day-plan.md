# 📅 The 90-Day Plan

### One quarter. Three goals. Measurable outcomes. Repeat four times a year.

---

## Why 90 days

A year is too long to plan — you'll abandon it by February. A week is too short to achieve anything meaningful.

**90 days is the sweet spot:** long enough to genuinely learn a framework or ship a real project, short enough that you can hold the whole plan in your head and actually feel urgency.

**Four of these a year. Sixteen across your degree.** That's the whole game.

---

## The rules

1. **Maximum 3 goals.** More than 3 means you achieve none of them.
2. **Every goal must be measurable.** "Learn React" ❌ · "Build and deploy 2 React apps" ✅
3. **Write down the weekly commitment**, not just the outcome.
4. **Review every Sunday** using [weekly-tracker.md](weekly-tracker.md).
5. **Don't change the goals mid-quarter.** Adjust the plan, not the target.

# 📚 Example plans

<details>
<summary><b>🌱 Year 1, Quarter 2 — Beginner</b></summary>

<br>

**Goal 1 — DSA:** Solve 80 problems (arrays, strings, hashing, basic recursion). Total: 20 → 100.
*Weekly: 7 problems, 5 hours*

**Goal 2 — Build:** Learn HTML/CSS/JS fundamentals and build + deploy a personal portfolio plus 3 small vanilla-JS apps.
*Weekly: 6 hours*

**Goal 3 — Theory:** Master OOP in my chosen language. Be able to explain all 4 pillars with my own examples.
*Weekly: 2 hours*

**Saying no to:** learning React, joining a second college club, competitive programming.

</details>

<details>

<br>

**Goal 1 — DSA:** Solve 120 problems covering trees, graphs and basic DP. Total: 180 → 300.

*Weekly: 3 hours*

**Saying no to:** starting a second project, learning a new framework, hackathons this quarter.

</details>
