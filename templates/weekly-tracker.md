## How to use this

1. **Fork this repo** *(ticking checkboxes only saves in your own fork)*
2. Copy this file to `my-tracker.md` in your fork
3. Duplicate the weekly block below for each new week
4. Fill it in every Sunday — 15 minutes, no more

**That's the whole system.** No app, no subscription, no elaborate Notion setup you'll abandon in three weeks.
