🧮 DSA & coding#

PlatformUse it forFree tier
LeetCode ⭐Your primary platform. Company tags, contests, discussionsExcellent. Premium optional
GeeksforGeeks Practice ⭐Indian company-specific questions, theory alongsideFree
HackerRankMany OAs run here — learn the interfaceFree
CodeChefIndian contests, good beginner laddersFree
CodeforcesCompetitive programming (only if you enjoy it)Free
InterviewBitStructured, timed, interview-shapedFree
AtCoderClean problem quality, good contestsFree
CodewarsGamified, good for language fluencyFree
ExercismLanguage mastery with human mentorshipFree

Tip

Practise on HackerRank at least a few times before your first OA. A large share of Indian company assessments run on HackerRank or HackerEarth, and their editors, input parsing and submit behaviour differ from LeetCode's. Fighting the interface during a timed test costs real marks.

Warning

Set an AWS billing alarm at ₹100 before you create anything. Destroy resources after every session (terraform destroy, terminate EC2 instances). Students genuinely receive ₹20,000+ bills from forgotten instances.

🎤 Mock interviews#

PlatformWhat it is
Pramp ⭐Free peer-to-peer mock interviews. Do these weekly
interviewing.io ⭐Free anonymous mocks with engineers from top companies
ExponentPractice + peer matching (some free content)
Friends / DiscordTrade mocks. Free and effective
Record yourselfSolve a problem out loud, watch it back. Painful, extremely effective

Important

Do at least 10 mock interviews before your real ones. The first time you feel interview nerves should not be in an interview that matters. Nerves are trainable — they fade with reps.

🏆 Contests & hiring challenges#

ContestWhenWhy
LeetCode Weekly / Biweekly ⭐Every weekTimed practice under pressure. Free
CodeChef StartersWeeklyBeginner-friendly Indian contests
Codeforces Div 3 / Div 4FrequentSpeed and problem-solving
TCS CodeVita ⭐AnnualGlobal contest with a direct interview path
Unstop challenges ⭐OngoingCompany hiring challenges — much less competition
HackerEarth challengesOngoingCompany-sponsored hiring contests
Google Kickstart / Code Jam archiveArchivedPractice problems from Google
Meta Hacker CupAnnualTop performers get contacted

Tip

Do LeetCode weekly contests even if you rank badly. Contest conditions — a clock, unfamiliar problems, pressure — are the closest free simulation of an OA that exists. Your rank doesn't matter; the reps do.
