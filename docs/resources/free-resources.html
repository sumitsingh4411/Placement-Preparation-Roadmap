Warning

Never pay ₹1–3 lakh for a "placement guarantee" course. Every one of them teaches exactly what's on this page, and takes your first year's salary for it. If a course promises a job, read the contract — it's usually an ISA (income share agreement) that costs far more than the sticker price. The only paid things genuinely worth considering are a ₹2,000 Play Store developer account and a cloud certification.
