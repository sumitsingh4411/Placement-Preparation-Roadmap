🧮 DSA Roadmap for Placement

How many problems you need, which sheet to follow, and the order to learn them in.#

🏠 Home • 📚 Full DSA guide • 🗺️ Roadmaps • 🎤 Interviews

Why DSA decides your placement#

DSA is the only universal filter. Every product company tests it, and it's the one part of hiring where your college genuinely doesn't matter — an online assessment is a text editor and some test cases.

It's also not about being smart. There are roughly 30 patterns. Once you've internalised them, most interview problems become "oh, that's sliding window with a hashmap." Getting there takes about 300 problems. There's no shortcut, and there doesn't need to be — it's just work.

How many DSA questions are enough for placement?#

TargetProblemsDifficulty mixTimeline
Service companies150–20070% Easy, 30% Medium4–6 months
Mid product / startups300–40040% E, 55% M, 5% Hard8–12 months
Strong product450–55025% E, 60% M, 15% H12–18 months
FAANG-level600–70020% E, 60% M, 20% H18–24 months

Important

300 problems you can re-solve beats 800 you copied from editorials. If you can't solve a problem again two weeks later without help, it doesn't count toward your total.

Which language should you pick?#

LanguagePick it ifNote
C++You want maximum DSA strengthBest STL, fastest execution, standard in contests
JavaYou're targeting Indian mass hiringMost common in service + enterprise job postings
PythonYou want the gentlest start, or lean data/MLCleanest syntax; occasionally restricted in OAs

Pick one and stay with it for 12 months. The language isn't the skill — the patterns are. Switching resets your muscle memory for no benefit.

The learning order#

Do not jump to dynamic programming in month two. DP without solid recursion is memorising solutions you'll forget in a week.

FOUNDATION (2-3 months)   Arrays · Strings · Hashing · Sorting · Searching · Recursion
        ↓
LINEAR (1-2 months)       Linked Lists · Stacks · Queues · Two Pointers · Sliding Window
        ↓
TREES (1-2 months)        Binary Trees · BST · Traversals · Heaps
        ↓
GRAPHS (1-2 months)       BFS · DFS · Topological Sort · Union-Find · Shortest Path
        ↓
DP (2-3 months)           1D · 2D · Knapsack · Subsequences
        ↓
ADVANCED (1 month)        Tries · Bit Manipulation · Segment Trees (optional)

📖 All 30 patterns with example problems: core/dsa.md

Which DSA sheet is best?#

SheetProblemsBest for
Striver's A2Z ⭐450Best all-round choice for Indian interviews. Free video solutions
NeetCode 150 ⭐150Best if you're short on time. Outstanding explanations
Blind 7575Absolute minimum before interviews
LeetCode Top Interview 150150Official and well-curated

Tip

Pick ONE and finish it. Students who jump between sheets solve the same 40 easy problems five times and never reach graphs and DP. Striver's A2Z if you have 8+ months; NeetCode 150 if you have less.

The method (this is the actual skill)#

The 25-minute rule:

TimeWhat to do
0–25 minTry genuinely. Brute force first — a working slow solution beats no solution
25–35 minHints only, not the full solution. Try again
35+ minRead the editorial. Understand why, not just what
ThenClose everything. Rewrite from memory. This is where the learning happens
+7 daysRedo it cold. If you can't, you never learned it

Every Sunday, re-solve 5 old problems from memory. This one habit is why some people retain 400 problems and others forget everything from three months ago.

Daily routine#

PhasePlan
Beginner (0–100)1 new problem (45 min) + revise 1 old
Building (100–300)2 new problems (75 min) + revise 2 old
Interview prep (300+)3 problems timed at 45 min each + 1 contest a week
Exam week1 easy problem. Never zero.

Never skip two days in a row. One skipped day is life. Two is the start of quitting.

Common questions#

How many DSA questions are enough for placement?

150–200 for service companies, 300–400 for mid product companies, 450–550 for strong product companies, and 600+ for FAANG-level roles. Quality matters more than raw count.

Which DSA sheet is best for placement preparation?

Striver's A2Z sheet if you have eight months or more, or NeetCode 150 if you have less time. Pick one and finish it rather than collecting sheets.

Which language is best for DSA?

C++, Java or Python all work. C++ is strongest for contests, Java has the most openings in Indian mass hiring, and Python is quickest to start with. Pick one and stay with it for a year.

Is DSA enough to get placed?

No. DSA gets you through the interview; projects get you the interview. You need both, plus CS fundamentals (OS, DBMS, networks) and — for service companies — aptitude. → What else you need

Two problems a day for a year is 700 problems. Nobody who did that stayed unplaced.#
