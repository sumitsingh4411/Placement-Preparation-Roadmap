🎓 B.Tech Placement Preparation

What to prepare, when to start, and how much is enough — for every year of your degree.#

🏠 Home • 🚀 Start Here • 🗺️ Roadmaps • 🎤 Interviews

The four things you prepare in parallel#

Most students do these one after another and run out of time. Do them together, in different daily blocks.

#WhatDaily timeWhy
1️⃣DSA45–90 minThe only universal filter. Every product company tests it
2️⃣One track + projects60 minGets you the interview in the first place
3️⃣CS fundamentals30 minOS, DBMS, CN, OOP — asked in every technical round
4️⃣Aptitude45 min (final year)Round 1 at every service company

Tip

Your syllabus already contains #3. OS, DBMS, CN and OOP are in your B.Tech course. Study them the way interviews ask ("when would you use X?") instead of the way exams ask, and you pass both from one effort.

When to start#

YearPriorityGuide
1stLearn one language properly. Basic DSA. Git + Linux. Target 150 problems.Year 1 →
2nd ⭐The make-or-break year. Pick one track, ship a deployed project, reach 300 problems.Year 2 →
3rdInternship, 2 portfolio projects, resume + LinkedIn, 500 problems.Year 3 →
4thStop learning new things. Apply 25+/week. Revise and interview.Year 4 →

Behind? There's a compressed plan for 4, 8 and 18 months → late-start.md

Important

Internship applications open in your 3rd year, around July–September — nearly a year before the internship itself. Most students discover this in March and find everything closed.

Which subjects matter for placement?#

SubjectInterview weightWhat they actually ask
Data Structures & Algorithms🔴 HighestLive coding, 45 min per problem
DBMS + SQL🔴 HighNormalisation, ACID, indexing, live queries
Operating Systems🟠 HighProcess vs thread, deadlock, virtual memory
OOP🟠 High4 pillars, abstract class vs interface, SOLID
Computer Networks🟠 Medium-HighTCP vs UDP, HTTP, DNS, "what happens when you type a URL"
System Design🟡 MediumOnly for strong product companies / 2+ YOE

The eligibility rules nobody tells you#

RequirementReality
CGPA above 7.0Many companies hard-filter at 6.5 or 7.0. Cheapest requirement to satisfy
Zero active backlogsMost service companies and many product companies require this at joining. A single carried backlog can void an offer
Consistent branch/degreeSome companies restrict by branch — check before you invest time

Warning

Backlogs are the silent career killer. Clear them immediately. Do not let one carry into your final year.

On-campus vs off-campus#

Your campus will bring 3–5 companies. India has thousands hiring freshers off-campus every year.

  • 300 applications → ~40 responses → ~12 interviews → 2–3 offers
  • Referrals convert ~10× better than portal applications
  • Start applying in 3rd year, not the final semester

📖 The complete system: placements/off-campus-strategy.md

Common questions#

How to prepare for placement in B.Tech?

Work on four things in parallel: DSA daily, one career track with real deployed projects, CS fundamentals, and aptitude if you're targeting service companies. Then apply off-campus at volume from third year onwards.

Which subjects are important for placement?

Data structures and algorithms, operating systems, DBMS with SQL, computer networks and object-oriented programming. All of these are already in your B.Tech syllabus.

When should B.Tech students start placement preparation?

Second year is ideal, because internship applications open in the third year. First year is even better if you can manage it. Third year still works. Final semester limits you mostly to service companies.

Is CGPA important for placements?

As a filter, yes — many companies cut off at 6.5 or 7.0, and most require zero active backlogs at joining. Above that threshold it stops mattering, and nobody asks after your first job.
