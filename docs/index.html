The situation

Nobody is coming to save you. That's the good news.

An online assessment is a text editor and some test cases. It cannot see your college's ranking. That is the crack in the wall, and it is wide enough to walk through.

What you're actually up against

  • Top companies never visit your campus
  • The placement cell brings three service companies at ₹3.5 LPA
  • Your syllabus was written before React existed
  • No seniors who've done it, no coding culture, no network
  • Everyone around you starts preparing in the final semester

What actually decides the outcome

  • Consistency — two focused hours daily beats fourteen every Sunday
  • Proof over claims — a deployed link beats “familiar with React”
  • Referrals — roughly 10× the callback rate of a portal application
  • Applying early — start in year 3, not the final semester
  • Explaining your thinking — silence loses interviews you technically passed

Step one

Pick one track. Go deep.

Depth is what gets hired. You can switch later — nobody's career is decided by their first job title. Eight full roadmaps, each with the stack, the projects, the interview rounds and the real salary range.

Know your target

Four levels. Apply to all of them at once.

Clear Level 1 as insurance, prepare for Level 2–3, and reach Level 4 by switching after 18–24 months of real experience. That's how most tier-3 engineers actually get there.

Level01

Service companies

TCS · Infosys · Wipro · Cognizant · Accenture · Capgemini · LTIMindtree — mass hiring, aptitude-first. Your safety net.

₹3.5–7 LPA~6 months prep

Level02

Mid product & startups

Zoho · Freshworks · Nagarro · Thoughtworks · funded startups — skill-based hiring, no college filter. Very achievable.

₹6–15 LPA12–18 months prep

Level03

Strong product companies

Razorpay · Zomato · PhonePe · Groww · Postman · Sprinklr · CRED — hard DSA plus system design. Your real target.

₹15–30 LPA~24 months prep

Level04

FAANG & equivalent

Google · Amazon · Microsoft · Adobe · Atlassian · Uber — off-campus only. Possible as a fresher, far more likely as a switch.

₹25–60 LPA24–36 months, or switch
